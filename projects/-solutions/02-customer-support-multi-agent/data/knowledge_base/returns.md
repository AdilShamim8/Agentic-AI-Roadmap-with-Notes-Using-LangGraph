# Returns & Refunds

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are in original condition and packaging. Digital goods and
gift cards are non-refundable.

To start a return, the customer needs their order ID. A prepaid return
label can be generated automatically once a return is approved. Refunds
are issued to the original payment method after the returned item passes
warehouse inspection, usually within 3-5 business days of receipt.

Damaged or defective items can be replaced immediately without waiting for
the returned item to arrive, at agent discretion, if the customer sends a
photo of the damage.
