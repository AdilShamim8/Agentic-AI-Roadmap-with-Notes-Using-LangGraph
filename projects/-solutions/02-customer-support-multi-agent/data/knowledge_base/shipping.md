# Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental US. Expedited
shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an extra fee.

If a package shows as "delayed", it usually means it is stuck at a regional
carrier hub. Delays of 1-3 extra days are common during peak season and do
not require a replacement order. If an order is delayed by more than 7 days
past its estimated delivery date, customers are eligible for a shipping
refund or a free re-ship, whichever they prefer.

Tracking numbers are provided by the carrier (UPS, FedEx, or USPS depending
on destination) once the order has shipped. Tracking can take up to 24 hours
to activate after a label is created.

We do not currently ship to PO boxes for items over 5 lbs.
