# Account Management

Customers can update their email, password, and plan from Account Settings.
Password resets send a reset link valid for 30 minutes.

Deleting an account is permanent after a 14-day grace period, during which
the customer can log back in to cancel the deletion. During the grace
period, active subscriptions are paused, not charged.

Plan upgrades take effect immediately and are billed pro-rata for the
remainder of the current cycle. Plan downgrades take effect at the start of
the next billing cycle.
