# Job archetypes

Based on analysis of 895 AI engineer job postings and 200+ agentic AI engineer postings on builtin.com (January-July 2026).

## The three job types

AI engineering jobs fall into three categories:

- AI-First: working ON AI systems directly (building agents, RAG, fine-tuning)
- AI-Support: working NEAR AI but not on it (platforms, infrastructure, tooling)
- ML: traditional ML/DL work without LLMs/agents (rebranded as "AI")

For agentic AI engineers, AI-First is the target. The breakdown:

### AI-First: 621 jobs (69.4%)

Working ON AI/ML systems directly.

What they build:

- Agentic workflows and multi-agent systems
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems
- Fine-tuned LLMs for specific domains
- Model serving and inference pipelines
- Prompt engineering and optimization
- Evaluation suites and CI-gated regression tests

Example responsibilities (from real postings):

- "Build agent workflows for customer support automation"
- "Implement multi-agent system for research and analysis"
- "Build RAG system for knowledge retrieval"
- "Deploy AI agents to production with monitoring"
- "Optimize prompts and agent performance"

Position title examples:

- AI Engineer
- Senior AI Engineer
- Applied AI Engineer
- Lead AI Engineer
- Staff AI Engineer
- Principal AI Engineer
- Agentic AI Engineer (emerging title, growing fast)
- AI/ML Engineer

### AI-Support: 255 jobs (28.5%)

Working NEAR AI but NOT ON AI itself.

These roles enable AI work by building the platforms, infrastructure, and tools that AI-First engineers use.

What they build:

- AI platforms and internal tooling
- GPU clusters and inference infrastructure
- Data pipelines for training/fine-tuning
- Frontend for AI products
- Deployment and monitoring systems

Position title examples:

- AI Platform Engineer
- AI Data Engineer
- AI Infrastructure Engineer
- Backend Engineer - AI Systems
- Full-Stack Engineer (AI/LLM Platform)

### Machine Learning: 16 jobs (1.8%)

Traditional ML/DL work without LLMs/agents. These "AI Engineer" roles are traditional ML roles rebranded with the AI title.

## How to tell the difference

The key question: Does this role work ON AI systems, or NEAR them?

AI-First (what agentic AI engineers want):

- Builds agent workflows
- Implements multi-agent systems
- Designs evaluation suites
- Optimizes prompts and agent architectures
- Deploys AI agents to production

AI-Support:

- Builds platforms for others
- Manages GPU infrastructure
- Builds data pipelines
- Creates deployment tooling
- Builds UIs for AI products

ML:

- Trains traditional ML models
- Works with structured data
- Builds computer vision systems
- Does NOT work with LLMs/agents

## The agentic AI engineer within AI-First

Within AI-First, there is a spectrum:

- RAG-focused: builds retrieval systems. 35.9% of AI-First roles. The entry point for many.
- Agent-focused: builds single-agent systems. ~30% of AI-First roles. The core of the agentic AI engineer role.
- Multi-agent-focused: builds multi-agent systems. ~10% of AI-First roles. The senior end of the role.
- Eval-focused: builds evaluation infrastructure. ~5% of AI-First roles. A specialty, often combined with agent-focused.

Most agentic AI engineer roles span RAG, agent, and eval. The multi-agent specialty is the senior career path.

## Customer-facing vs. internal

- Customer-facing roles: 25.8% of AI engineer postings. You build agents that serve end users.
- Internal-tooling roles: 74.2%. You build agents for internal teams.

Agentic AI engineer roles skew more toward customer-facing (around 40%), because agents are often customer-facing products (support, research, coding assistants).

## Research vs. applied

| Role Type | Jobs | % |
|----------|-----:|--:|
| Research | 39 | 4.4% |
| Applied/Production | 856 | 95.6% |

95.6% of AI engineer roles are applied, not research. Agentic AI engineer roles are even more skewed toward applied (less than 2% are research). This is a production engineering role, not a research role.

## Key insight: AI-First + agents + evaluation = agentic AI engineer

The agentic AI engineer is a subset of AI-First that specifically works on agents (not just RAG) and evaluation (not just deployment). If a posting mentions LangGraph, multi-agent systems, or evaluation suites, it is an agentic AI engineer role. If it only mentions RAG and prompt engineering, it is a broader AI engineer role that may not give you the agentic experience you want.
