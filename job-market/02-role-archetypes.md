# Role archetypes

See [field guide chapter 3](../field-guide/03-job-archetypes.md) for the full treatment of AI-First, AI-Support, and ML role archetypes.

## Summary

- AI-First (69.4% of AI engineer postings): works ON AI systems. This is where agentic AI engineers live.
- AI-Support (28.5%): works NEAR AI but not on it (platforms, infrastructure, tooling).
- ML (1.8%): traditional ML work rebranded as "AI engineer."

For agentic AI engineers, AI-First is the target. Within AI-First, the agentic AI engineer is the subset that specifically works on agents (not just RAG) and evaluation (not just deployment).

## How to identify the archetype in a posting

Read the responsibilities, not the title. An "AI Engineer" posting might be any of the three archetypes.

- If the responsibilities mention "agents", "multi-agent", "LangGraph", or "tool calling" - it is AI-First, agentic.
- If the responsibilities mention "RAG", "embeddings", or "vector database" but not agents - it is AI-First, RAG-focused (not agentic).
- If the responsibilities mention "platform", "infrastructure", or "GPU" - it is AI-Support.
- If the responsibilities mention "PyTorch", "training", or "model architecture" - it is ML (rebranded).

## The agentic AI engineer premium

Agentic AI engineer roles command a salary premium of 10-20% over broader AI engineer roles at the same level, because the agent-specific skills (LangGraph, multi-agent, evaluation) are scarce. See [field guide chapter 7](../field-guide/07-salary-and-career-trajectory.md) for the full salary data.

## Where agentic AI engineers work

Based on the 200+ agentic AI engineer postings analyzed:

- AI-native startups (LangChain, Anthropic, OpenAI, Scale, Hugging Face, Cursor, Perplexity, Glean, Sierra). The cutting edge.
- Enterprise AI teams (Capital One, GEICO, Thomson Reuters, etc.). Building internal tools.
- Consulting firms (Deloitte, McKinsey, BCG). Building agents for clients.
- Platform companies (Vercel, Cloudflare, AWS). Building agent infrastructure.

The AI-native startups are where the most interesting work happens. The enterprise teams are where the most stable jobs are. The consulting firms are where the variety is. The platform companies are where you build the tools other agentic AI engineers use.
